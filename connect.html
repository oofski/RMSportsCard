How to connect

  1. Open your phone’s Camera app and point it at a QR code above.
  2. Tap the link that pops up.
  3. Your phone will say the connection “isn’t private.” That’s expected for a local app on your own network. Tap through it once:
    • iPhone (Safari): Show Details → “visit this website”.
    • Android (Chrome): Advanced → “Proceed anyway”.
  4. When the app opens, tap the browser’s Share → Add to Home Screen so next time it’s one tap — no QR needed.
Advanced: remove the security warning permanently

Optional — the steps above already work. To never see the warning again on a phone, install this computer’s certificate as trusted on that phone:

  1. Download the certificate (open this page on the phone first, then tap the link).
  2. iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → install the profile. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn it on for “RM Cardz Inventory”.
  3. Android: Settings → Security → “Install a certificate” → CA certificate → pick the downloaded file.
